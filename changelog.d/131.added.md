`GET /threads/{thread_id}/runs/{run_id}/stream` — join a run's event stream (SDK `runs.joinStream`, `useStream` `reconnectOnMount`). Runs created with `stream_resumable: true` buffer their SSE events, with the ids the original stream carried, for the run's lifetime plus `SkeinoSettings.resumable_stream_ttl_seconds` (default 600); a join replays the events after `Last-Event-ID` (`-1` = from the start) and then tails live events. Honours `stream_mode` and `cancel_on_disconnect`.
